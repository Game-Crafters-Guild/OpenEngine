The grass pictures in this folder, 144 x 96 PNG each (shown at 72 x 48 logical pixels), are crops of
the editor's Scene View, taken through its render-graph readback (take_screenshot --target viewport,
a 3067 x 900 frame).

Scene: the Basic3D project template with a terrain added (Terrain BaseSource Flat, 256 x 256 m
centered on the origin, no material) and TerrainGrass at its defaults. Sky Environment:
TimeOfDayHours 10, AnimateTimeOfDay false; the Post Process Volume as the template ships it.
Scene View camera: position (0, 0.15, -131.5), 3.5 m outside the terrain's edge at z -128, yaw 90
(facing +Z, into the field), pitch 3, vertical field of view 60 degrees, the view's default auto
exposure; gizmos off. The field is seen side on, so the blades stand against the sky and their height
reads at the picture's size.

grass-<preset>.png: the preset picked from the Preset list. A 360 x 240 crop with its top-left
corner at (1300, 282) in the frame, scaled to 144 x 96 (Lanczos) with no gain. The crop ends on the
terrain's front edge, above the gray ground past it, and its top row holds Steppe's tallest tips.

Build: 037e7e83a5. Retake a picture when its preset's values, or Ground Tint Height's default, change.

The values each picture was taken with, one line per picture: the preset picked, the gain, the
preset's dials (colors as the stored sRGB word in hex, root auto for the automatic root color), then
Ground Tint Height, which no preset sets but which changes how each preset looks.
TerrainGrassPresetRowTests compares these lines to the preset table, so a preset whose values change
fails the test until its picture is retaken and its line updated.
