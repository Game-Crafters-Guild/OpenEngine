The star pictures in this folder, 144 x 96 PNG each (shown at 72 x 48 logical pixels), are crops of
the editor's Scene View, taken through its render-graph readback (take_screenshot --target viewport,
a 3067 x 900 frame).

Scene: Main.scene of the Basic3D project template. Sky Environment: TimeOfDayHours 23, MoonArcPosition 0
(no moon), AutoMoonArc false, AnimateTimeOfDay false; the Post Process Volume as the template ships it.
Scene View camera: position (0, 2, 0), yaw 90, pitch 50, vertical field of view 60 degrees, the view's
default auto exposure.

The look pictures, star-look-<look>.png, are one window of the frame: a 48 x 32 crop with its top-left
corner at (1606, 270), a bright blue-white star with an orange and a blue star beside it, scaled 3x
(Lanczos) with no gain, so the bright star's glow, spikes or sparkle show at the picture's size. Each is
the look picked from the Preset list, Seen by Camera so the stars keep their colors.

The Seen by pictures, star-seen-by-<eye|camera>.png, are their own window: a 72 x 48 crop with its
top-left corner at (1660, 92), sixteen stars, most of them orange or blue and fainter than magnitude
3.5, where Eye fades color to white, scaled 2x (Lanczos) with no gain. Each is the Vivid look with Seen
by picked on the tile. With Eye the faint stars turn white; with Camera they keep their colors. The
window is denser than the look window and zoomed less, so many stars change color at the picture's size.

Build: 99f2273701. Retake a picture when its look's values change.

The values each picture was taken with, one line per picture: the look picked, Seen by, the gain, then the
look's star dials and night tint. StarLookPresetTests compares these lines to the preset table, so a
look whose values change fails the test until its picture is retaken and its line updated.
