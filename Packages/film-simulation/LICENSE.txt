FidelityFX Lens-derived film grain
----------------------------------

The film grain shader (Assets/Shaders/film_simulation_grain.frag) adapts the
FidelityFX Fast path and the PCG3D16 generator of AMD FidelityFX Lens 1.1, from
https://github.com/GPUOpen-LibrariesAndSDKs/FidelityFX-SDK. The license below is
LICENSE.txt of that repository at v1.1.3 (54fbaafdc34716811751bea5032700e78f5a0f33),
copied verbatim.
