title: Bloom, scattering and cinematic halation
description: What the Bloom Effect's Intensity, Diffusion, and Spread do, how Film Simulation's halation differs, and how the passes compose and filter.
---

Bloom has one additive intensity and a separate Diffusion amount. Cinematic halation belongs to Film Simulation and has an independent highlight source, radius, tint, and intensity. None of these controls changes another's meaning.

| Control | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Intensity | Gain of thresholded highlights, added after filtering. Zero adds no highlight bloom. |
| Diffusion | A soft, threshold-free haze of the frame mixed over the whole image, as a fraction (0–1). It is energy-conserving: it moves light rather than adding it. Zero turns it off and skips its passes. |
| Spread | How far highlight bloom reaches, from a tight glow to a wide halo. It shapes the Diffusion haze the same way and changes neither amount. |
| Film Simulation → Halation | Highlight halo with its own amount, full-resolution-pixel radius, and HDR tint. Works with Bloom Effect absent or disabled. |

## Composition

```text title="The frame after bloom, scattering and the depth veil"
mix(scene, blurredScene, scatteringAmount) + intensity × tint × blurredHighlights + depthVeil
```

Scattering preserves constant fields and approximately preserves integrated scene light away from screen boundaries. Additive highlights, depth veil, lens dirt, and film halation are artistic additions and can increase energy. Finite HDR range and edge clamping limit this screen-space approximation.

## Cinematic halation

Halation extracts highlights above one exposed display white from the original HDR scene, before downsampling, using a soft knee and a bounded HDR contribution. It does not read the scattering pyramid, bloom tint, intensity, threshold, or depth veil. Ordinary midtones therefore do not acquire full-screen red haze. A normalized separable Gaussian spreads the source. Radius changes spread, not total halo energy. Its three passes are skipped when halation is off.

## Filtering and cost

Bloom thresholds in exposed units before the wide prefilter. Anti Flicker adds a shared-RGB, neighbor-relative soft shoulder for isolated hot samples, preserving hue. Diffusion bypasses this nonlinear suppression to retain its energy contract. Both pyramids use normalized Gaussian downsampling and positive cubic B-spline reconstruction. Eight independently weighted octaves use weights `k^(5 × Spread − 2.5)`, normalized after the last-octave fade.

Radius selects active depth up to Octaves. The next octave is allocated only above a 0.001 fractional fade, remapped continuously from zero. Lens dirt can request the full highlight pyramid. Diffusion uses a separate pyramid. The highlight pyramid is skipped when additive highlights and depth veil are off. Halation adds independent extraction and two blur/composite passes. Shader fetch counts are not a GPU timing claim. Release timings must be measured on the target scene and hardware.

## Verification

Production GPU tests cover constant-field Diffusion, additive gain, distinct-octave numerical reconstruction, radius continuity, small colored highlights, hot samples, halation-only rendering, exposure-relative extraction, midtones, and halo spread/energy. Structural graph tests complement pixel tests. These do not substitute for visual review and Release timings on shipped content or a Steam Deck hardware run.
